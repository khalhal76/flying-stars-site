Feng shui software for consultants

Last updated 9 October 2026

Flying Stars prepares the calculating part of a client study: the Xuan Kong chart from the bearing and the completion date, the nine sectors fitted to the client’s own floor plan, and a room-by-room reading you reword before it goes out. The judgement stays yours.

What does a consultant actually use it for?

The part of a study that is arithmetic rather than experience. You enter the facing bearing and the date the building was completed or last renovated; the period, the mountain, water and period stars of all nine sectors follow from those two inputs.

From there you fit the nine sectors to the walls of the client’s plan by hand, add the residents and their birth dates, and work through the rooms. Each room page asks where the door and the windows are, then gives the placement for the bed, the desk, the cooker and the seats, with the reason for every wall chosen or ruled out.

Does it replace the consultant’s judgement?

No, and it is not built to. A floor plan cannot show the land, the surroundings, the approach to the building, the state of the neighbours’ roofs or anything about the people beyond their birth dates. All of that is the consultant’s work, and none of it is in the file.

What the software removes is the chance of an arithmetic slip in a chart you have drawn a hundred times, and the hour spent typing the same explanations into a document. The same building, entered by any two consultants, produces the same chart.

Can I change the wording before the client sees it?

Yes. Every line of the reading can be rewritten on Pro before it goes into the report, so the document reads in your voice rather than the app’s. The calculated figures stay as they are; the prose around them is yours.

The report prints the floor plan with its sectors, the residents and their directions, and a page for each room, in the order you arrange them on the project sheet.

How are client projects kept apart?

Each project is its own record, held in your account, and readable only by the account that made it. Floor plans, names and birth dates are stored so the project opens on your other devices. Deleting a project removes them, and you can delete the whole account from inside the app.

Pro belongs to the account rather than the device, so a study started on a laptop at the office opens on a tablet at the client’s home.

Does it handle offices and shops?

Yes. A commercial project reads the plan for the head of the business, the managers, finance, sales, reception and the other roles, and the report is worded for an office rather than a household.

What does it not do?

It does not add the yearly or monthly stars, and it does not draw a replacement-star chart. When a bearing sits within 1.5 degrees of a mountain boundary it says so and asks you to measure again rather than choosing a side for you. It makes no promise about health, money or relationships.

These are deliberate limits. A tool that quietly picked a side on a boundary case, or printed a prediction, would be making a judgement that belongs to the practitioner standing in the building.

What does it cost?

The free plan covers the main floor plan, one room page, one resident and one saved report — enough to put a real client plan through it before paying. Pro is US$19.99 a month or US$99.99 a year, and adds every room and resident, furniture on the plan, rewording the report, unlimited reports and a library of projects. You can cancel at any time and Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.

Where does it run?

In any browser, and installed from the browser on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Chromebook. On Windows it is in the Microsoft Store as an installed app. Your projects follow the account, not the machine.
