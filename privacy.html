Flying Stars Feng Shui — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026 · App: Flying Stars Feng Shui (com.simplefoods.flyingstars) · iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and the web

Flying Stars Feng Shui helps feng shui consultants prepare a flying star study of a home: the floor plan, the people who live there, and advice room by room. This page explains what the app keeps, where it is kept, and how to delete it.

Your account

The app works with an account, so your projects are yours wherever you sign in — on an iPhone, an iPad, a Mac, an Android device, a Windows PC or a web browser. You can sign in with your email address, using a one-time code we send you, or with Sign in with Apple. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address; we then only receive a private relay address from Apple.

For the account we keep your email address (or the relay address) and a random account number. We do not ask for your name, phone number or payment details.

What is kept in your account

So that a project can be opened on any of your devices, everything you put into it is saved to your account:

  • floor plans and room photos or PDFs you add, and any area cut out of them
  • the property's name, build or renovation date and entrance direction
  • the names, dates of birth, gender and household roles of the residents you add, which are used to work out each person's number and best directions
  • furniture you place, and any wording you change in the report
  • how many reports have been saved on the free plan

A copy is also kept on each device you use, so the app opens quickly and works while you are offline. The report pages you save go wherever you choose to save or share them.

Your devices

Your account works on one phone or tablet and one computer at a time. So that we can hold those two places open for you, we keep a record of each device you sign in on: a random identifier for the device, whether it is a handheld or a computer, the name the device reports, and the date it was added. It is not used for advertising and never follows you into other apps. You can see both devices, and remove one to free the slot, under Your devices in the app.

How it is protected

Your account and projects are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider. Data travels encrypted between the app and the servers, and is encrypted where it is stored. Each project can only be read by the account that created it. We do not look at the contents of your projects, and we never sell or share them.

About the people in a study

If you are a consultant, the residents you add are usually your clients and their family. You are responsible for having their permission to record their details and for how you share the reports you create. Deleting a project removes their details from your account.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat. In the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps, payment is taken by Apple or Google. In the Windows app and on the web, payment is taken by card through Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record, which receives your card details, your email address and your billing country to take the payment, prevent fraud and meet tax rules, under Paddle's privacy policy. Either way, we never see your card details. RevenueCat receives your random account number and the purchase record, so that a subscription bought on one device is recognised on your other devices. Neither receives anything from your projects.

There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in this app, and nothing is used to follow you across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting

  • Delete a project — from the project library. It is removed from your account and from your devices.
  • Delete your account — in the app, under your account. Your account, every project and every picture are deleted from our servers straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are gone within 30 days.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription — in the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription by itself.

We keep your data only for as long as your account exists.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is kept or who receives it, the effective date above will change and the updated policy will be published here before the change reaches the app.
