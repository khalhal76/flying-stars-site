Install Flying Stars on any device

Flying Stars runs in the browser, and every device below can also install it with its own icon and window. Sign in with the same account and your projects and your plan are there.

iPhone and iPad Mac Windows Android phone or tablet Chromebook

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open flyingstarsfengshui.net/app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.

Step-by-step help for iPhone and iPad

Mac

  1. Open flyingstarsfengshui.net/app in Safari.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File, then “Add to Dock”, then “Add”.

Flying Stars gets its own icon in the Dock and opens in its own window. This needs macOS Sonoma (14) or later.

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar instead.

Windows

Get Flying Stars from the Microsoft Store.

Get it from Microsoft Store

Or open the web app in Edge or Chrome and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Android phone or tablet

  1. Open flyingstarsfengshui.net/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots at the top right).
  3. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”, then “Install”.

A Google Play version is in testing.

Chromebook

  1. Open flyingstarsfengshui.net/app in Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu (the three dots) and choose “Install Flying Stars Feng Shui”.

It then sits in the launcher and the shelf like any other app.
