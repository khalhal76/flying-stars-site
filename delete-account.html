Delete your Flying Stars Feng Shui account and data

App: Flying Stars Feng Shui (com.simplefoods.flyingstars) · Developer: Khaldoun Al Hallak · Last updated: September 22, 2026

You can delete your Flying Stars Feng Shui account, and everything kept in it, from inside the app at any time. You do not need to ask us, and nothing is kept back.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Flying Stars Feng Shui and sign in, if you are not signed in already.
  2. Go to the project library — the screen that lists your projects.
  3. Under your email address, tap delete account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm with Delete my account.

That is all. The account is deleted straight away, on every device you have signed in on.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can no longer sign in, email khaldoun.hallak@simplefoodsme.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We delete the account and confirm by reply, normally within 7 days and at the latest within 30 days.

Reports you have already saved

A report you saved or shared outside the app — a file on your device, or one you sent to a client — is yours and is not touched by deleting your account. Delete those copies yourself.

Your subscription is separate

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first:

  • Android — Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • iPhone, iPad or Mac — Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Windows or the web — the upgrade screen in the app, Manage subscription, or the link in your receipt from paddle.net.
