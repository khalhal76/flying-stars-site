Flying Stars on iPhone and iPad

Flying Stars runs in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen and it gets its own icon and opens full screen, like any other app. It takes three taps.

  1. Open the web app in Safari Go to flyingstarsfengshui.net/app.
  2. Tap Share The square with an arrow pointing up. On iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen; on iPad it is at the top right.
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add” Scroll down the list a little if you do not see it at first.

Flying Stars now sits on your Home Screen. Open it from there, sign in, and your projects are waiting, the same ones you see on a computer.

Questions

Do I need the App Store? No. Everything in Flying Stars works this way: the floor plan, the chart, the rooms, the people and the report.

What about Pro? Upgrade from inside the app with a card. Pro belongs to your account, so it works on every device you sign in to.

Another browser? Chrome and Edge on iPhone can do the same from their Share menu. Safari is the simplest.

Your account and projects are covered by the same privacy policy and terms as everywhere else.
