How it works

1 · The plan

Add a photo or a PDF of the floor plan. Enter the period the building was completed in and the bearing of its entrance, in degrees. Fit the nine sectors to the walls by hand. The plan itself is never altered.

2 · Room by room

Give each room its own page. Mark the door and the windows, then place the furniture: the head of the bed, the desk, the cooker, the seats at the table.

3 · The report

Reword any line in your own language and save the finished report — the plan, the rooms and your notes — ready to give to the client.

Worked out, not invented

Every figure comes from the classical Xuan Kong flying star method. The bearing in degrees selects one of the twenty-four mountains, the completion date selects the period on the solar calendar, and the mountain, water and sector stars of all nine sectors follow from those two inputs. The same building, entered by any two consultants, produces the same chart.
