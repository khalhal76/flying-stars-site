How it works
  1. Face out. Stand inside the front door, looking out. Take the compass reading.
  2. Date the home. The date it was built or renovated sets the period.
  3. Upload the plan. Fit the nine sectors to its walls.
  4. Add the people. Birth dates give each person their best directions.
  5. Go room by room. Mark doors and windows. Place bed, desk and cooker.
  6. Save the report. Reword any line first.

Worked out, not invented

Bearing in degrees→1 of 24 mountains Completion date→The period →All nine sectors

Every figure comes from the classical Xuan Kong flying star method. The bearing in degrees selects one of the twenty-four mountains, the completion date selects the period on the solar calendar, and the mountain, water and sector stars of all nine sectors follow from those two inputs. The same building, entered by any two consultants, produces the same chart.

Flying Stars applies the classical Xuan Kong method to your floor plan — the chart and every placement are calculated, not guessed. A floor plan cannot show everything: the land, the surroundings, the people. For important decisions, an experienced practitioner’s eye is worth having.
